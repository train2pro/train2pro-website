Who we are

Train2Pro is a training business run by Khanjan Kadi, based in India. This policy explains how we handle personal information on this website and on the Train2Pro Learning Portal (train2pro.khanjankadi.com). Contact: train2pro@khanjankadi.com, +91 99099 65218.

What we collect

  • Contact details you give us: name, email address, phone number, organisation and role
  • Your answers and results from the DISC test, and Thomas International PPA reports where you buy one
  • Your learning activity on the Learning Portal: programmes, progress, quiz scores and certificates
  • Order details for purchases. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by our payment partner, Razorpay; we do not see or store them

How we use it

  • To provide the programmes, reports and coaching you sign up for
  • To suggest programmes that fit your DISC style and role
  • To send course updates, reminders and, if you agree, news about new programmes and webinars
  • To issue receipts and certificates and to meet legal and accounting requirements

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only where needed to run the service: our payment partner (Razorpay), our website and video hosting providers, Thomas International when you take a PPA assessment, and your employer or sponsoring organisation for corporate or CSR programmes. Your individual DISC report is shared with your employer only with your consent.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and learning records while your account is active and for up to three years after, unless you ask us to delete them sooner or the law requires us to keep them longer.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop marketing messages, at any time by writing to train2pro@khanjankadi.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page. Last updated: 17 November 2026.
