Online programmes

All programme purchases are final. Fees are not refunded, whether or not the programme has been started or watched. Please read the programme details and try the free DISC test before you buy.

Bundles

Student Starter and Professional Growth bundles are not refundable. Coaching hours in a bundle can be rescheduled under the coaching rule below.

One-on-one coaching

You can reschedule a session free of charge with at least 24 hours' notice. Sessions missed without notice are not refunded.

Thomas International PPA reports

Once the assessment link has been sent, the PPA fee is not refundable, because the licence cost is paid to Thomas International at that point.

Corporate, college and CSR programmes

Cancellations and refunds follow the terms in the signed proposal or agreement for that programme.

Payment problems

If you were charged twice or charged without getting access, email train2pro@khanjankadi.com with your name and order number, or WhatsApp +91 99099 65218. We reply within 2 working days and return any wrong charge to the original payment method through Razorpay; banks usually take 5 to 7 working days to show the credit.
