What is DISC?

Understand Behaviour. Transform Performance.

DISC is a scientifically validated behavioural assessment used by over 50 million people worldwide. It identifies your natural behavioural tendencies across four dimensions to improve communication, teamwork, and leadership effectiveness.

D

D – Dominance

How you respond to problems and challenges

Results-oriented Direct Decisive Competitive

High D individuals are direct, results-driven, and thrive in environments that require quick decisions and bold action. They are natural problem-solvers and change agents.

I

I – Influence

How you influence people and situations

Enthusiastic Persuasive Collaborative Optimistic

High I individuals are expressive, people-oriented, and thrive when engaging and inspiring others. They bring energy, creativity, and enthusiasm to teams and projects.

S

S – Steadiness

How you respond to pace and consistency

Reliable Patient Supportive Team-focused

High S individuals are loyal, consistent, and thrive in stable environments. They are excellent listeners, natural collaborators, and the backbone of high-performing teams.

C

C – Conscientiousness

How you respond to rules and procedures

Analytical Accurate Systematic Quality-focused

High C individuals value quality, accuracy, and logic. They excel at analysis, process design, and ensuring high standards — bringing rigor and expertise to every task.

Our Process

How the DISC Assessment Works

1

Complete the Assessment

A validated 20-minute online questionnaire. No right or wrong answers — just honest self-reflection.

2

Receive Your Profile

Get a detailed 25–30 page Personal Profile Analysis (PPA) covering your style, strengths, and motivators.

3

Debrief Session

A 1-on-1 or group debrief with a certified facilitator to explore your profile and develop action plans.

4

Apply & Grow

Use DISC insights in real-world contexts — hiring, team dynamics, coaching, and leadership development.
